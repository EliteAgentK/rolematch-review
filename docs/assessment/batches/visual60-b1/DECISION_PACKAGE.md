## What improved, what worsened, what is unmeasured

* **Improved (accepted):** E1 publication of CVs whose names wrap (7 more protected CV documents).
* **Improved but not accepted:** E2 recovers listed skills in every format and, downstream, removes all under-crediting against verified inputs for Word and text (669 criterion leaves each) and most for PDF, scan and photo, with **no new unjustified credit** at leaf level. It also admits fragments from interleaved OCR columns as skills on photos and scans: an end-to-end extraction error even where it earns no credit against these jobs.
* **Worsened by E3:** two correct skills lost (development); unsupported names not reduced.
* **Unchanged by all three:** unjustified credit from reading order on PDF, scan and photo (205, 320 and 481 leaves): skills interleaved into duty lines are credited as described use, and misread role titles count as relevant experience. Refusals: 4 PDF, 6 scan, 17 photo CVs of 60.
* **Unmeasured:** assessor correctness (no independent criterion labels); fresh evaluation (none available); real scans and photographs; recruiter workload.

## Release decision

* **Recommended scope:** none beyond the current default (parser 2.9.0 with E1). Formula A remains offline only, and Formula B disabled.
* **Rollback target:** parser 2.9.0 (commit 228852a) is already the default. E2 and E3 are off.
* **Owner approval needed for:**
  1. the review-budget tie policy: in 43 of 60 jobs the whole-tie rule includes over twice the budget, so it removes the workload benefit on pools where most applicants are out of role;
  2. whether the "unsupported names" gate should be per format, in total, or a rate, prospectively for future experiments only;
  3. any normalisation policy for R23.
* **Promotion evaluation is blocked:** this batch has no fresh protected data left that has not informed development.
