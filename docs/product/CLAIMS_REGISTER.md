# Claims register: what the public pages say, what is true, and what must be proved

Owner rule (2026-10-22): the platform must contain everything the public pages promise, and **every claim must be proved true**. Until a claim is proved, the page must not make it. This register lists each claim on the Home, Features and Pricing pages (`src/pages/HomePage.jsx`, `FeaturesPage.jsx`, `PricingPage.jsx`), says what is true today, and what proof would justify it. Nothing here has been changed on the pages yet.

Verdicts: **True** (can stay, with the wording given) · **Reword** (true in part) · **Build, then prove** (the owner wants the feature; the claim waits for it) · **Remove** (conflicts with a platform rule or the law; see the reason).

What counts as proof: a test in the repository that fails if it stops being true; a measurement on stated data, recorded with its date and sample; or a document (a licence, a legal opinion). A number on a page links to the measurement that produced it. Demo charts must say they are illustrations or use real data.

## How this is kept true

1. **One source.** This register is the list of allowed claims. A page may only state what is marked True or Reword here.
2. **A guard.** When the pages are corrected, a test will fail the build if a removed phrase returns ("unprecedented", "cultural fit", "Feature benefit point", "reduce bias" without its proof, and the rest of the Remove rows).
3. **Proof before words.** A "Build, then prove" row moves to True only when its test or measurement is linked here, with date and sample size.
4. **Dates.** Every figure on a page carries the date and data it came from.
