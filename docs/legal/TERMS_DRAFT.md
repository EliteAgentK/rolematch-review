## 1. What RoleMatch does

RoleMatch reads CVs and job descriptions, keeps them in the Customer's own portals, and recommends how well candidates match jobs, with the evidence
and how confident each recommendation is. **RoleMatch recommends; the Customer's people decide.** RoleMatch does not reject, shortlist, interview or
hire anyone, and the Customer remains responsible for its recruitment decisions.

## 2. Accounts

One e-mail address is one account, belonging to one company. The Customer's administrators decide who has access to which portal and are
responsible for removing people who leave. Passwords and sign-in codes must be kept private.

## 3. The Customer's information

- The Customer owns what it uploads. RoleMatch uses it only to provide the service to that Customer, as its operator under the data processing
  agreement (which forms part of these terms).
- The Customer confirms it may lawfully give RoleMatch the CVs it uploads, and tells candidates how their information is used (a ready-made
  candidate notice is available).
- Each Customer's information is kept apart from every other Customer's.

## 4. The AI

RoleMatch uses an outside AI service, outside South Africa, to read documents and assess matches (see the privacy policy and the data processing
agreement). AI can be wrong: every recommendation shows its evidence so a person can check it. Facts read from a document may be corrected where the
Customer's settings allow; what RoleMatch works out is never presented as certain.

## 5. Fair use

The Customer will not upload anything unlawful or harmful, try to reach another Customer's information, test RoleMatch's security without written
permission, or use RoleMatch to decide about people without human review. RoleMatch may limit use that endangers the service for others.
