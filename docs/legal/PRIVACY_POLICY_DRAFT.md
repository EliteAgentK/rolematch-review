## 2. Two kinds of information, two roles

- **Your account.** When you or your company signs up, we are responsible for your account information and decide how it is used.
- **Candidates' CVs.** When a company uploads CVs to RoleMatch, that company decides why and for which jobs. The company is responsible for that
  information; we process it only on its instructions, as its operator, under a written agreement. If your CV was uploaded by a company, that
  company is the one to ask first about your information, and you may also contact us.

## 3. What we collect

| From whom | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| People who sign up or are invited | Name, work e-mail address, company, role, password (stored only in scrambled, one-way form), sign-in times, when you last used RoleMatch | To run your account, keep it secure, and show your company who has access |
| Candidates, through the company that uploads their CV | The CV file and everything it states: name, contact details, work history, education, skills, and anything else written in it | To read the CV and recommend matches to jobs, for the uploading company |
| Companies | Job descriptions, recruiters' notes and decisions, workflow status | To run the company's recruitment on RoleMatch |
| Everyone using the site | Technical records: the time of a request, what failed, and short browser descriptions for security | To keep RoleMatch working and secure |

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

## 4. How the AI is used

RoleMatch uses an AI service to read CVs and job descriptions and to assess how a candidate matches a job.
**The AI recommends; people decide.** RoleMatch never rejects, shortlists, interviews or hires anyone by itself. Every recommendation shows its
evidence and how confident it is, so a person can check it (POPIA section 71).

## 5. Where your information is kept and sent

- **Kept in South Africa.** RoleMatch's database and the original CV files are kept on RoleMatch's own servers in Johannesburg, encrypted.
- **Sent outside South Africa.** To read a CV or job description and to assess a match, the text is sent to our AI provider,
  **[Anthropic, PBC, United States: owner's decision 2]**, which processes it and returns the result. Under our agreement with it, it may use the
  information only to provide this service to us and does not use it to train its models **[confirm against the provider's current commercial
  terms before publishing]**. We rely on POPIA section 72: the provider is bound by an agreement that gives protection substantially similar to
  POPIA, and the transfer is necessary to provide the service the company asked for.
- **Other services that handle information for us:** Cloudflare (it carries traffic between your browser and RoleMatch, worldwide) and
  **[the e-mail service behind mail.rolematch.co.za]** (it sends RoleMatch's e-mails).

## 7. How we protect it

Each company's information is kept apart from every other company's, enforced in the database itself. Original files are encrypted with keys kept
away from the database. Access by RoleMatch staff to a company's records is limited, recorded, and shown to that company in its own log. Sign-in
attempts are limited. If information is accessed by someone who should not have it, we will tell the affected company, the Information Regulator
and, where required, you, as soon as reasonably possible (POPIA section 22).
