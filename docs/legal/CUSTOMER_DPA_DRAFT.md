# Data processing agreement between a customer and RoleMatch (DRAFT, not live)

*Draft of 12 Nov 2026. The operator agreement POPIA section 21 requires, for the CVs a customer uploads. This is the document a corporate's legal
and procurement teams ask for first. To be reviewed by an attorney before use.*

**Parties:** the Customer (the *responsible party*) and **Natus Lab [registered name as on the CIPC certificate]** ("RoleMatch", the *operator*).

## 1. What RoleMatch processes, and why

| | |
|---|---|
| People concerned | Candidates whose CVs the Customer uploads; people named in job descriptions; the Customer's own users |
| Information | Whatever the CVs and job descriptions contain (names, contact details, work history, education, skills, and anything else written in them); recruiters' notes, decisions and statuses |
| Purpose | To read the documents, keep them in the Customer's portals, and recommend matches for the Customer's people to review |
| Instructions | RoleMatch processes the information only to provide the service as the Customer uses it, and never for its own purposes, advertising, or training AI models |

## 2. RoleMatch's commitments (POPIA sections 19 to 22)

1. **Confidentiality.** Only people who need to, under a duty of confidentiality, may access the Customer's information. RoleMatch support opens a
   portal's records only when the Customer asks, for one hour at a time, recorded, and shown to the Customer in its own log.
2. **Security.** Each customer's information is kept apart in the database itself; original files are encrypted with keys kept apart from the
   database; access is limited and recorded; sign-in attempts are limited; backups are encrypted.
3. **Breaches.** RoleMatch tells the Customer without undue delay, and within **[48 hours]** of becoming aware, of any access to the Customer's
   information by someone not allowed it, with what is known, so the Customer can meet its own duty under section 22.
4. **Requests from people.** RoleMatch helps the Customer answer people's requests to see, correct or delete their information.
5. **Deletion.** The Customer can delete documents and candidates at any time; RoleMatch removes the original, the text read from it and the record
   together. On ending, RoleMatch deletes the Customer's information after **[30 days]**, except billing records (no CV contents) kept 7 years.
6. **Audit.** RoleMatch gives the Customer the information reasonably needed to show it keeps these commitments.

## 3. Outside companies RoleMatch uses (sub-operators)

| Company | What it does | Where | Information it receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Anthropic, PBC: decision 2]** | Reads CVs and job descriptions and assesses matches (AI) | United States | The text of documents being read or matched, for that request |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Carries traffic between browsers and RoleMatch | Worldwide network | Everything in transit, encrypted |
| **[E-mail provider for mail.rolematch.co.za]** | Sends RoleMatch's e-mails | **[where]** | Recipients' e-mail addresses and the e-mail |
| RoleMatch's own servers | The database and the encrypted original files | Johannesburg, South Africa | Everything |

RoleMatch binds each by written agreement to protection substantially similar to POPIA's, tells the Customer before adding or replacing one, and
the Customer may object.

## 4. Information leaving South Africa (POPIA section 72)

The AI provider processes the text of documents in the United States. RoleMatch relies on section 72(1)(a) (the provider is bound by an agreement
giving substantially similar protection) and 72(1)(c) (the transfer is necessary for the service the Customer asked for). The Customer tells its
candidates, for example with RoleMatch's candidate notice.

## 5. Decisions by machine (POPIA section 71)

RoleMatch makes no decision about a person by machine alone. Every recommendation shows its evidence and confidence, and only the Customer's people
decide.
