# Machines and scheduling

> **Corrected 6 November 2026 (owner: "the Mac is free just for model fine-tuning").** The live model moves to the PC's processor. The Mac only trains, and keeps its backup copy on disk. There is no training window: live never waits for training. The window procedure below applies only if the PC cannot read CVs fast enough and the owner chooses to switch back. See [the review](17_REVIEW_AND_IMPROVEMENTS.md) §3.5.

Basis: ADR-004 §9, deploy/limits.sh, deploy/mac/run.sh, deploy/pc/daily-check.sh and the backup scripts. PC Dell Precision 5820 has 16 GB RAM, WSL capped at 10 GB; Mac M5 has 16 GB. Brief reports PC containers about 4.3 GB of the WSL allowance; sampling scope and peak unverified. Mac runs Ollama/training and stores backup copy only. Do not treat one-time measurements as spare capacity. PC hosts app, database, private vault, queues and rehearsals, with explicit separate volumes, ports, credentials and resource caps.

Before training window: announce planned delay, stop new model dispatch, finish or checkpoint in-flight model work; continue durable intake within quotas. During: show “Waiting for document analysis” when needed, keep existing panels and authorised deterministic fallback available under its explicit state, do not silently downgrade or claim completion. At deadline stop training, verify pinned inference health, resume fair dispatch by lease and measure drain time. Schedule outside demonstrated peak and Mac backup collection; initial exact hours require owner decision and measurement. Timeouts, STOP, disk caps and exclusive locks apply. If model recovery fails, keep queue durable and alert owner; protect interactive workload.

For purchased server: measure workload first; choose architecture supported by pinned images or build multiarch; restore DB plus vault and keys, exercise TLS, tunnel, ACLs and restore, then freeze/delta/switch per 12. Keep data and configs portable; validate customer-cloud topology before claiming compatibility. No purchase or system change authorised here.
